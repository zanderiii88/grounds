# City maps — 1.25.0

Six revised city day/night pairs form the entire location catalogue. Artwork retains its natural aspect ratio and registers in a 1200-unit native coordinate frame. A shared stadium scale of 0.87 applies to east [7.2,4.2] and south [-7.35,4.2]; the SVG viewport uses 1100 scene units. Fixed pitch anchors prevent upgrades from moving the ground.

Each background has an affine correction with x unchanged and y'=c(x-originX)+d(y-originY)+originY. This aligns average ground-plane slopes while preserving upright verticals. A raster's four irregular plot edges cannot all be made parallel by that correction alone.

Therefore the final visible plot and apron are SVG polygons generated from the same ground axes as the stands. Their four edges are mathematically parallel to those axes. The replacement apron covers the original painted boundaries. Its paving grid also follows the engine axes. Day/night scenes use identical plot geometry with different material colours. This does not certify every distant generated road/building edge as exact.

Maximum stand depth 14.79, rear hospitality depth 2.6 and access allowance 0.4 produce a conservative ground envelope x=2.21..69.79, y=2.21..57.79. The plot adds 2.5 ground units of clearance in each direction. The exterior apron covers the measured artwork plot plus a six-unit allowance and was reviewed in all six settings, by day and night.

Figure glyphs multiply by (site.scale*830/site.artWidth)/0.6137143383204945 to preserve their preceding release's size relative to the stadium. Routes and seat positions continue to use engine projection.

Camera frames follow actual stand bounds. Floodlights, pedestrian routes and selection volumes do not determine stadium zoom. Close views use meet; menus use dedicated desktop/mobile framing with a background upper-edge clamp. All startup composites are generated by the browser from sceneSvg.
