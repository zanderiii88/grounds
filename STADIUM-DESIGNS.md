# Clubline grounds — design direction

These are fictional grounds. The named real venues are references for stand arrangement and character, not copies of their plans, branding or exact capacities. Starting club capacities range from 20,000–55,000. Player edits change a career ground’s capacity within 10,000–75,000.

| Club | Ground | Seats | Direction | Architectural cue |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Calderwick Athletic | Foundry Lane | 55,000 | Large main stand, imposing home end, mixed tier heights, brick exterior | Anfield’s expanded Main Stand and Kop contrast |
| Inverbrae Rovers | Riverside Ground | 52,000 | Two towering adjacent stands, lower opposite sides, glass roof edge | St James’ Park’s asymmetrical skyline |
| Greyhaven United | Harbour Park | 49,000 | One steep single-tier end against multi-tier sides, continuous canopy | Tottenham Hotspur Stadium’s South Stand |
| Ironfield Works | Railway End | 43,000 | Four distinct stands, prominent brick main stand and visible trusses | Ibrox’s traditional Main Stand character |
| Dunbrae Thistle | Thistle Park | 39,000 | Three taller joined sides and a smaller fourth stand | Celtic Park’s redevelopment pattern |
| Abercelyn City | Celyn Arena | 36,000 | Even multi-tier structure and continuous structural canopy | Principality Stadium’s strong roof structure, scaled down |
| Fenleigh Albion | Meadow Road | 32,000 | Characterful home end and brick-faced main stand | Villa Park’s Holte End identity |
| Portcairn Athletic | Portcairn Ground | 29,000 | Modern, tight four-stand footprint, cantilevered roofs | Brentford Community Stadium’s compact form, scaled up |
| Crowmere Town | Crowmere Park | 26,000 | Steep close-to-pitch stands and tight corners | Tynecastle Park’s intimate atmosphere |
| Cwmderyn AFC | Cwm Field | 24,000 | Balanced modern bowl, even roofline | Swansea.com Stadium’s uniform bowl |
| Ballyrath United | Rath Lane | 22,000 | Mixed old and new single-tier stands | Windsor Park’s redevelopment of three stands alongside an older side |
| Strathford FC | Brae Park | 20,000 | Modest separated stands with open corners and one uncovered end | Traditional smaller Scottish grounds |

Release 1.22.0 retains the original modular anchors and club layouts. Standard and steep singles, overhang and setback doubles, and triple tiers use the approved structural renderer. Corner sections use their own stand family on a quarter-circle footprint, with no neighbour-driven morphing. Exposed tier supports remain stepped; rear concourse ends are closed separately. Four roof styles provide different fascia thicknesses and supports. Setback depth is restored to its approved dimensions, with a maximum of 14.79 engine units before rear blocks. Existing background plots do not yet accommodate every maximum layout. Capacity estimates remain tied to each club's initial ground; this visual pass does not rebalance capacities.
