# Current artwork — 1.25.0

Only Aberdeen / Granite Harbour, Liverpool, Manchester, Cardiff, Dublin and Birmingham are included, each by day and evening. The surrounding city artwork was revised around the stadium footprint and preserves each location's established character. Night variants were generated as lighting edits of the revised day maps.

The final plot and apron are engine-drawn SVG surfaces over registered artwork. This prevents generated plot-edge drift and retains exact stadium axis directions. See CITY-MAP-NOTES.md for registration and clearance details. Distant generated road and building edges can still have local irregularities.

Approved branding and modular stadium geometry are retained. Startup images are actual browser-rendered engine composites. No old location assets, traffic or specialised courtyards are included.
