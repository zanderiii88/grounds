# Clubline — Release 1.7.0

Copy the contents of this ZIP to the root of your GitHub Pages repository. The update check reads `version.json`. Existing 1.x careers can continue; new statistics start recording from matches played in this build.

## This release

- Stadium corners blend their roof height and depth into adjacent stands, with curved exterior shells and stepped exposed ends. This removes the flat full-height caps and dark corner wedges shown in the supplied screenshots. Day and evening scenes use the same geometry.
- Starting XI includes formation, play style and team orders. Changing formation preserves the selected players and lets you rearrange them; Auto pick remains optional. The separate Formation, Play style and Orders tabs are removed.
- Each player tile has an individual-instruction control and a five-state happiness face. Player details explain the reason. Playing time, squad role and an occasional higher demand for minutes affect happiness. Substitute appearances help; a very unhappy player can request a loan or transfer listing.
- Managers can offer an outgoing season loan, subject to maintaining at least 18 players. The player returns after the final fixture. Daily attention highlights player requests and transfer offers.
- A sortable Statistics page shows club or Premier Division players. It tracks starts, substitute appearances, minutes, goals, assists, overall rating, average match rating, goalkeeper goals conceded and clean sheets. Other clubs' individual numbers are generated when their fixtures resolve.

## Notes

The site art and parked vehicles have not been rescaled in this release. The league remains one 12-club Premier Division with 22 fixtures per club. Local career saves are kept in the same key.
