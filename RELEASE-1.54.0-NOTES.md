CLUBLINE 99 — FULL RELEASE 1.54.0 / CLUB LIFE & STADIUM EVENTS

Based on the verified complete 1.53.0 release. Nothing deployed.

IMPLEMENTED
20 original club stories, covering charity visits, dressing-room disagreements, supporters' fundraising, local sponsorship, settling-in support, guest coaching, transport repairs, school visits, supporters' evenings, donated equipment, wet-weather training, newspaper rumours, celebrations, community gardens, testimonials, volunteers, shop repairs, player recognition, team meals and office disruption. Some resolve as news; others offer two decisions. Choices show their direct effects before confirmation, including cash, club morale, community goodwill, player happiness and healthy-player condition. Reports record the actual bounded changes and affected players. Paid choices require sufficient cash; a departed player cannot receive a targeted effect. Unanswered decisions expire after seven days without extra spending or bonuses.

Initial stories start after 18–27 elapsed days. Subsequent stories are spaced 21–32 days apart, avoid matchdays, allow at most one unresolved story, and exclude the previous eight story types. Stadium proposals begin after 25–36 days and subsequently arrive 38–52 days apart. Rendering/reload never rerolls generation. All event state and repeatable outcome seeds belong to the saved career.

Three booking types: summer stadium concerts (May–August), community football days and corporate hospitality evenings. Offers show expected visitors, gross income, costs, net income, the response deadline and all reserved days. Concerts reserve one setup day and two recovery days; community days reserve the event plus one recovery day; corporate evenings reserve the event date. Whole booking windows are checked against home fixtures, other accepted bookings and current construction. Accepted bookings prevent later conflicting home friendlies and construction. Away fixtures do not occupy the stadium. Proposed bookings expire if unanswered. Accepted bookings can be cancelled before setup without money movement.

Income and operating costs settle once on the event date, using repeatable modest variations from the estimate. Reloading, advancing again or restoring a backup cannot pay the same event twice. A newly conflicting fixture causes safe cancellation without income or costs. Calendar markers include setup/event/recovery days. Existing accepted off-season bookings are processed during season rollover; advance remains available after the last match while a booked event/recovery window is outstanding. New bookings are not generated during the off-season jump.

Organiser → Club life & stadium events contains proposals, decisions and reports. Home links to this screen and shows club morale/community goodwill. News notifications link to event review; routine event reminders can be disabled under Options. Morale starts neutral at 50/100, has a capped ±2-point influence on the managed team's match strength, and does not replace player ratings. Community goodwill starts at 50/100 and contributes at most ±5 points to supporter confidence, with the reason shown in the assessment.

Original reusable SVG event assets use the stadium engine's actual projection: concert stage/canopy/screen/lights, visitors with restrained movement and a service truck; simple stalls and visitors for community days; tables and visitors for corporate evenings. Objects sit within the stadium pitch area; existing stands, worlds and street geometry are retained. Event artwork appears on the event date only and clears when advancing beyond it. It is separate from the cached stand surfaces and never appears in a football match. Reduced-motion mode pauses event SVG motion and disables the light/screen CSS animation.

PRESERVED
The approved start artwork, four menu buttons, audio under Options, all 20 fixed neighbourhood scenes, all kits and the 1998–99 league data. Assets/data are byte-identical to 1.53.0. Modular stand geometry, stadium surface cache, live-match scene retention, wage planning and opposing formation comparison are preserved. Save key and backup format remain unchanged. New event-state validation rejects malformed event backups; older careers receive neutral event state without forced stories or money changes.
